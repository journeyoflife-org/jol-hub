The `data/` module composes four cooperating layers around a shared `src/config.py` registry of GDPR Article 30 processing activities: (1) `airflow/dags/` schedules daily/weekly ETL and reporting jobs that invoke Python entry points; (2) `src/pipelines/` performs country-specific syncs, bulk entity imports, and k-anonymized donation aggregation; (3) `dbt/models/` and `sql/` transform raw tables through staging → intermediate → mart layers while enforcing PII masking and retention expiry; (4) `src/gdpr/` provides the cross-cutting pseudonymizer, retention manager, and ROPA generator consumed by both pipelines and dbt models. All layers share the same database connection settings, retention policies (`RetentionPolicy`), and classification enum from `src/config.py`, and all outputs are validated via Great Expectations expectations under `src/quality/expectations/`. The `exports/ropa/lt/` directory is the canonical artifact produced by the ROPA generator for Lithuanian entities, and `tests/` enforces the no-Stripe dependency boundary across all layers.
