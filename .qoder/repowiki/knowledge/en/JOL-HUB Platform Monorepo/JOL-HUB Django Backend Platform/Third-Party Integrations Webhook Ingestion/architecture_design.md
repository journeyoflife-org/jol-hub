Django app `apps.integrations` exposes DRF `APIView`s under `/api/v1/integrations/webhooks/` (PayPal, Bitrix24, Bitrix24 health) that accept unauthenticated POSTs, perform source-specific signature/idempotency checks, store raw payloads in MongoDB via `apps.core.mongodb.WebhookPayloadCollection`, and enqueue Celery tasks for async processing. `models.py` defines two PostgreSQL audit models — `WebhookEvent` (state machine: pending → processing → processed/failed/ignored with unique `idempotency_key`) and `OutboundRequest` (outbound call audit). `tasks.py` implements Celery `@shared_task` handlers with explicit transient vs permanent error classification driving autoretry/backoff; `process_bitrix24_webhook` follows a fixed state-machine pipeline (fetch from MongoDB → get_or_create tracking record → early-exit on already-processed → mark PROCESSING → execute business logic → mark PROCESSED or FAILED) and delegates entity sync to `_sync_contact` / `_sync_lead`. Business logic is isolated in `bitrix24_mappings.py`, which centralizes field maps (`CONTACT_FIELD_MAP`, `LEAD_FIELD_MAP`), allowed-field whitelists (`ALLOWED_CONTACT_FIELDS`, `ALLOWED_LEAD_FIELDS`), event-type routing tables (`EVENT_ENTITY_MAP`, `EVENT_OPERATION_MAP`), and transformation helpers (`extract_email`, `extract_phone`, `parse_bitrix24_date`, `parse_decimal`, `map_source_id`, `mask_pii`, `validate_fields`, `detect_consent_from_fields`). Dependency direction is one-way: views → tasks → mappings → external apps (`apps.crm.models`, `apps.organizations.models`, `apps.core.mongodb`); the app has no internal sub-packages beyond these flat modules plus `migrations/` and `tests/`.
