Standard Django app under `apps.financial` with a flat MVC layout: `models.py` defines `Invoice` and `Payout` (both extending `apps.core.models.BaseModel`) linked to `Organization`; `serializers.py` exposes them via DRF `BaseModelSerializer` subclasses; `views.py` provides DRF `ListAPIView`/`RetrieveAPIView` endpoints filtered by `organization_id` and soft-delete flag; `urls.py` mounts routes under the `financial` app namespace. Both models override `save()` to enforce tenant isolation by calling `_validate_tenant_context()`, which lazily imports `apps.crm.middleware.get_current_tenant_id` and raises a `ValidationError` when the target organization does not match the current tenant — a security boundary enforced at the persistence layer. The app depends on `apps.core`, `apps.organizations`, and optionally `apps.crm` (import is wrapped in try/except so the app remains usable without CRM middleware).
