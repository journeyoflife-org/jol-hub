Standard Django app under `apps.donations` providing a single domain model `Donation` (in `models.py`) that extends `BaseModel` from `apps.core.models` and links to `Organization` and the auth user. The app exposes three DRF view classes in `views.py`: `DonationListCreateView` (list/create with per-user scoping), `DonationDetailView` (retrieve), and `DonationRefundView` (stateful refund operation). URL routing is declared in `urls.py` under the `donations` app namespace and mounted at `/api/v1/donations/`. Serializers in `serializers.py` split read (`DonationSerializer`, mostly read-only) and write (`DonationCreateSerializer`, auto-fills donor fields from the authenticated request). Admin registration lives in `admin.py`. Cross-tenant isolation is enforced inside `Donation.save()` by importing `get_current_tenant_id` from `apps.crm.middleware` and raising a `ValidationError` when the donation's organization does not match the active tenant. Refunds are wrapped in an atomic transaction that updates the donation status and creates a tamper-evident `AuditEntry` (from `apps.crm.models`) recording actor, IP, timestamps, and field changes. Throttling is delegated to shared classes `DonationCreateThrottle` and `DonationRefundThrottle` in `apps.core.throttling`. Migrations live under `migrations/` and define the initial schema.
