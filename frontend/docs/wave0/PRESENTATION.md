## Demo script (25 minutes, 5 sites × 5 min)

### 1. Diocese (bishop's office) — lead with authority
1. Open `siauliai-diocese.gyvenimo-kelias.lt` on a phone, then mirror to screen.
2. Show the stats block (67 parishes / 7 deaneries) and the deanery index.
3. **Emphasize:** one platform, every parish gets a site; the diocese keeps
   the overview. Switch LT → EN live (locale switcher).
4. Close with the bishop's letter page — "your voice, published in minutes."

### 2. Cathedral — lead with beauty
1. Hero + Mass schedule (Sunday family Mass highlighted).
2. Events: organ evening, family Sunday — the living parish calendar.
3. Donations page — **demo mode**: explain the payments boundary opens
   after PCI SAQ A (ADR-007); no fake checkouts shown.
4. Heritage page — history as evangelization.

### 3. Funeral home — lead with dignity
1. Calm tone, 24/7 call-out CTA first (the moment of need).
2. Transparent pricing list — families hate surprises.
3. Booking: choose a consultation slot (test mode — confirm the email).
4. Obituaries with GDPR Art. 9 consent flow — "we protect the departed's
   data like our own."

### 4. Cemetery care — lead with professionalism
1. Stats block (640 graves maintained) builds trust in 5 seconds.
2. Care plans with monthly/annual pricing + photo reports.
3. **Live booking demo** — pick a date, get confirmation.
4. For cleaning-company owners: "your clients book you at 11 pm on Sunday."

### 5. Kražiai church — lead with affordability
1. Two-page site, warm copy, Mass times — nothing superfluous.
2. **The price reveal:** CHEAP tier runs a full parish site for pocket
   money; upgrades are one click, never a rebuild.
3. "Even the smallest parish deserves a dignified presence."

## Q&A preparation

| Question | Answer frame |
| --- | --- |
| **Pricing?** | Three tiers (CHEAP/NORMAL/VIP); diocesan volume agreements; no setup fee during Wave 0 |
| **Timeline?** | Reference sites live now; parish onboarding in days, not months |
| **Customization?** | Tenants control the designated content blocks through the moderated editor; JOL keeps the security perimeter |
| **Support?** | LT-native support, runbooks, 90-day onboarding window |
| **Data ownership?** | Parish data stays parish data — GDPR Art. 9 handling documented, export/erasure guaranteed |
| **What happens to donations?** | Payments open after PCI SAQ A verification; test mode until then — never before compliance |

## One-pager content (per tier)

- **CHEAP** — "Dignity for every parish": site + schedule + contacts,
  read-only content, community pricing.
- **NORMAL** — "A living parish": + news/events, contact CRM, bookings,
  moderated content editing.
- **VIP** — "The full cathedral": + commerce, donations, priority support,
  custom domain, diocesan dashboards.

## Contact collection

Interest form on each demo site's `/contact` (routes to the CRM test
pipeline). Collect: name, institution, tier of interest, preferred demo
date. **Never** collect sacramental or donor data in demos — fixtures are
anonymized by design.
